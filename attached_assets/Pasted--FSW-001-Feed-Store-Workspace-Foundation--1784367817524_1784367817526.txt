# =====================================================
# FSW-001 — Feed Store Workspace Foundation
# =====================================================

OBJECTIVE

Build the foundation for the Feed Store Workspace.

This Workspace represents feed stores and suppliers that sell livestock feed, feed ingredients, supplements, minerals, vitamins and commercial feed products.

This task creates the foundation only.

DO NOT implement ordering.

DO NOT implement checkout.

DO NOT implement inventory synchronization.

DO NOT implement payment.

DO NOT implement shipping integration.

Use realistic dummy data only.

----------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE

Prepare the architecture for Feed Store Workspaces that integrate with Marketplace and Farm Workspaces.

----------------------------------------------------

SUPPORTED PRODUCTS

Prepare support for:

- Grass & Forage
- Silage
- Hay
- Concentrate
- Complete Feed
- Feed Ingredients
- Feed Additives
- Premix
- Mineral
- Vitamin
- Milk Replacer

Architecture only.

----------------------------------------------------

Read-only.

Read-only.

No functionality.

Architecture only.
