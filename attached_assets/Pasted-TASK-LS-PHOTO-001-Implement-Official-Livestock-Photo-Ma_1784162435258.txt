==========================================
PHOTO MODEL
==========================================

Each livestock has ONLY three official photo categories.

1. FOTO IDENTITAS
- Taken when born OR
- First entered into farm (offline purchase)
- Used by:
  - Livestock List
  - Profile
  - KTP
  - Marketplace
- Exactly ONE active photo.

Replacing:
Old photo automatically moves to history.

Never delete.

==========================================

2. FOTO PRESTASI

Examples:

- Kontes
- Juara
- Sertifikat
- Pameran
- Lelang
- Event resmi

Unlimited.

Always append.

Never replace previous records.

Newest first.

==========================================

3. FOTO TERBARU

Represents current livestock condition.

Exactly ONE active photo.

Replacing it:

Previous photo moves into history.

Never delete.

==========================================
PHOTO HISTORY
==========================================

Every replaced photo becomes immutable history.

History stores:

photo_uuid

livestock_uuid

photo_category

taken_date

uploaded_date

uploaded_by

reason

notes

replaced_by

History cannot be edited.

History cannot be deleted.

==========================================
AUDIT TRAIL
==========================================

Create immutable audit entries.

Examples:

Photo Archived

Each audit contains:

action
